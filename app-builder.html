NEMAWASHI HOSTING

Your free Nemawashi address

https://nemawashi.hrmnx.site/app/...

Before connecting a custom domain, every website has its own Nemawashi path.

1. Add the domainEnter the domain you want to use.
2. Add CNAMEPoint the requested host to nemawashi.hrmnx.site.
3. Verify DNSWait for DNS propagation, then verify the CNAME record.
4. Enable HTTPSNemawashi's hosting layer must issue and validate the TLS certificate.
5. PublishAfter verification, publish the website to the custom domain.
