HOW IT WORKS

Your idea, in four steps.

01

Build a Music Brainstorm

Develop your production, lyrics and creative direction in an InDesign Music Brainstorm.

02

Publish a public DEM

Import your .prodem and .lyrdem packages into your programme Music Brainstorm, sync the lyrics, then publish your DEM.

03

Submit it here

Publishing a public DEM from your official programme project submits it automatically.

04

Become competition-eligible

Only DEMs submitted through this official programme can be selected for a Producer Competition.

A quick note

Only public DEMs published from projects created in this official Producer Programme workspace become eligible. Ordinary public DEMs are not eligible.
